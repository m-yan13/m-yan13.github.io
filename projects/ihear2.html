iHEAR2++

A low-cost portable acoustic measurement system built around synchronized multi-channel acquisition.

FOCUSHardware + embedded systems
CORESTM32H723VGT6
STATUSTested + measured
PROJECT RECORD

OVERVIEW

Designed a portable acoustic measurement system using a five-microphone array and an STM32H723VGT6 control PCB. The system synchronously acquires and transfers five channels of PDM microphone data to a PC.

TESTING

Compared measured noise reduction from multi-acquisition averaging against the theoretical expectation. Across three recordings, averaging produced 3.85× noise reduction, corresponding to 11.71 dB.

SIGNAL INTEGRITY

Investigated data-clock interference and used shielded cabling together with source-series termination to reduce crosstalk by approximately 80%, bringing interference below the target threshold.

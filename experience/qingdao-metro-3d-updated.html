Qingdao Metro-3D

Electronic Testing / Quality Assurance Engineering Intern Qingdao, China May 2024 – Aug 2024
Testing Modeling

During my internship at Qingdao Metro-3D, I worked with coordinate measuring machines (CMMs) to support dimensional inspection of parts produced for automotive applications. My day-to-day work involved preparing measurement setups, calibrating CMM machines and probes, carrying out dimensional measurements, and reviewing the resulting data for accuracy and consistency. The work provided practical experience with how precision measurement is performed in an industrial quality assurance environment.

A major part of the role involved setting up different probe configurations for specific measurement tasks. CMMs use interchangeable probes and probe orientations to access different surfaces and features of a part, so the appropriate configuration had to be selected and installed before measurement. After setting up a probe, I worked through the calibration process using measurement artifacts with known geometric dimensions. The calibration established the probe's position and geometry relative to the CMM so that subsequent measurements could be referenced accurately within the machine's coordinate system.

I also worked with spherical calibration artifacts and other known reference features when preparing CMMs for measurement. The calibration process began with a manual probe measurement to establish the location and orientation of the calibration artifact. The CMM could then use that reference to locate and automatically measure the remaining calibration points. This allowed the machine to establish the probe's effective geometry and compensate for the specific probe setup before measuring production parts.

Once a machine and probe configuration were calibrated, I used RationalDMIS to carry out dimensional inspections. Measurements were performed at the micrometer scale, with different CMM models and probe configurations used depending on the inspection requirements. I recorded measurement results and compared repeated measurements to identify discrepancies between setups and calibration conditions. When differences were observed, I investigated the measurement process and repeated calibration or setup procedures as needed to improve consistency.

The role also involved supporting the operation of air-bearing systems used by the CMM equipment. I assembled custom air-pressure control systems using electronic pneumatic regulators to provide controlled air pressure for the machine's air-bearing operation. This gave me additional exposure to the interaction between mechanical measurement equipment, pneumatic systems and electronic control components.

WHAT I LEARNED

This experience gave me a practical understanding of precision measurement beyond the theoretical concepts encountered in coursework. I learned how probe selection, calibration, machine setup and measurement procedure can affect the reliability of dimensional results, and how repeated measurements and discrepancy analysis can be used to identify sources of error. Working with CMM equipment and automotive parts also strengthened my understanding of quality assurance, measurement processes and the importance of repeatability when working with precision engineering systems.

How I approach engineering

I enjoy engineering problems where the answer is not immediately obvious. My approach is to break a system down into physical and technical questions, test assumptions, and use measurements and analysis to understand what is actually happening.

My project experience spans electronic hardware, PCB design, embedded systems, robotics, controls, and system testing. I am particularly interested in roles where I can work on practical systems and continue developing my engineering skills alongside experienced engineers.
